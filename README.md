# Short Side Trainer

A browser-based trainer for learning and practicing intraday short selling, for both small caps and liquid large caps.

- **Learn:** 10 lessons covering borrow and locates, SSR, LULD halts, dilution, small-cap and large-cap setups, and risk management, plus a quiz
- **Simulator:** synthetic 1-minute sessions with halts, SSR, locates, a simulated SPY, and coach feedback
- **Size Calc:** position sizing from your stop distance, with a halt/gap stress test
- **Setup Grader:** a checklist that scores a setup before you trade it
- **Journal:** tracks R-multiples, expectancy, and stats by setup, with an equity curve (stored in the browser's localStorage)

For learning only, not financial advice. Simulator price data is synthetic.
